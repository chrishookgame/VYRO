# VIDEO MODEL

Version: 1.0

---

## PURPOSE

The Video model represents every video published on the VYRO platform.

This model is the foundation for:

- Feed
- Video Player
- Search
- Recommendations
- AI
- Analytics
- Marketplace
- Monetization

---

## RESPONSIBILITIES

A Video must contain:

- Identity
- Creator information
- Media information
- Statistics
- Publication date

---

## RULES

1. Every video has one unique ID.
2. Every video belongs to one creator.
3. Every video has one playable source.
4. Every video can accumulate statistics.
5. Every future module must use this model.
